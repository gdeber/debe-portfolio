I designed and helped operate the voting platform used during X Factor, where traffic could surge sharply during live broadcasts. The system was built to handle peaks of 75,000 requests per second.

I applied SRE principles to performance, resilience, and observability so the service could remain dependable under exceptional demand. The experience informed the team's approach to operating high-visibility services and preparing for unpredictable load.

Due to legal reason I'm involved in operating it during on-air show from Repower Theater in Assago.
