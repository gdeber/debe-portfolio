At SET Sviluppo, I developed a production tracking system that followed pipes through their full lifecycle, from manufacturing to shipping. The system improved production data accuracy and provided a competitive advantage in international markets.

My focus was on realtime operations, PLCs and production system integrations. 

The solution supported operations in Italy, Romania, Saudi Arabia, and Kazakhstan.
