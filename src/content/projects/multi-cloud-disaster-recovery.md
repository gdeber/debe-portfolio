I designed and implemented a disaster recovery system for Sky's multi-cloud infrastructure. Its goal was full reconstruction across all channels, with a single VM serving as the recovery starting point.

The process brings the infrastructure back in under two hours, reducing recovery complexity while providing a practical path to restore services across AWS and GCP.
