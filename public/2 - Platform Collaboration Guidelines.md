# 2 \- Platform Collaboration Guidelines

*How to work with and within the Platform Ecosystem*

## 1. Purpose

The platform exists to **empower teams** — removing friction, ensuring consistency, and enabling faster innovation. Everyone using the platform is part of the **ecosystem** and can help it grow.

---

## 2. Principles for Working with the Platform

### **a. Understand Before You Request**

- Before opening a request, explore:
    - Platform documentation
    - Existing APIs or workflows
- Often, the capability you need already exists or can be composed from what’s available. → **Empower yourself first; escalate when blocked.**

### **b. Problems, Not Solutions**

- When requesting something new, describe the **problem** and **outcome you need**, not just a feature idea.
    - ❌ “Add a new upload endpoint.”
    - ✅ “We need a faster way to process video metadata during ingestion.” This allows the platform team to design a **solution that scales** across use-cases, not a one-off fix.

### **c. Early Collaboration**

- Involve the platform team **early** in your initiative, especially if:
    - It introduces new tech or integrations.
    - It changes workflows shared by other teams.
    - It might require infrastructure or data model updates. → Early discussions reduce rework and ensure platform alignment.

### **d. Feedback Loops**

- Use structured channels for feedback (e.g., Slack, Jira, or service portal).
- Share both **pain points** and **success stories** — both shape priorities.
- Participate in regular **Platform Showcases or Reviews** when available.

---

## 3. Becoming a Co-Creator

You’re not just a user — you’re part of the **platform ecosystem**.

### Ways to contribute:

- **Share reusable patterns**: If you build something generic, offer it back as a template or plugin.
- **Document insights**: Add usage examples, best practices, or FAQs.
- **Join open sessions**: Participate in backlog reviews and showcases. 
- **Advocate standards**: Help enforce shared models and APIs across your domain.

### Co-creation mindset:

> “If it helps me, and could help others — it belongs on the platform.”

---

## 4. When to Build Outside the Platform

Building independently may be appropriate when:

- The use-case is **experimental or domain-specific**.
- You need **speed to validate** before requesting platform adoption.
- It doesn’t require shared infrastructure, data, or governance.

If the experiment succeeds → **bring it back** to the platform for standardization and scale.

---

## 5. Culture of Partnership

- The platform team’s role: **empower and standardize**.
- The ecosystem teams’ role: **innovate and give back**.
- Together we build a **resilient, scalable, creative ecosystem** — where collaboration replaces dependency.
