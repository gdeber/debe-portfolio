# 1 \- Platform Manifesto

## DRAFT  
Our Definition

A **platform** is not a product: Products deliver value *to* users. Platforms deliver value *through and with* users and partners.**  
**It is a **foundation that empowers others** to build, extend, and create value.  
Its success depends on the **ecosystem it enables**.

## Guiding Principles

### 1. **Ecosystem First**

- Our platform exists to **create value through and with others**, not just deliver features directly.
- Every design choice must consider **developers, creators, partners, and users (partecipants).**

### 2. **Abstraction with Power**

- We **remove complexity** (infrastructure, operations, repetitive tasks) while keeping the flexibility for advanced use-cases.
- Abstraction should **accelerate, not restrict**.

### 3. **APIs Are Products**

- APIs, SDKs, and interfaces are **first-class citizens**.
- They must be **well-designed, documented, stable, and versioned**.
- A broken API is a broken trust.

### 4. **Openness with Governance**

- Platforms thrive on **extensibility** — plugins, integrations, customizations.
- Openness must be balanced with **clear governance**, ensuring security, compliance, and fairness.

### 5. **Self-Service by Default**

- The best platform is one where **teams move without waiting on us**.
- Self-service tools, documentation, and sandboxes are our default mode.

### 6. **Scalability and Reliability**

- A platform is a **critical dependency**.
- It must be **resilient, observable, and scalable** so others can innovate without fear.

### 7. **Network Effects over Features**

- Success is measured not by how many features *we* ship, but by how much value the **ecosystem creates** on top of us.
- Encourage adoption, collaboration, and co-creation.

### 8. **Shared Responsibility**

- Everyone who interacts with the platform — engineers, product managers, business teams — has a role in its growth.
- **Platform team provides the foundation; ecosystem teams provide the innovation.**

---

## For the Platform Team

- Build with **API-first mindset**.
- Think in **reusable primitives, not point solutions**.
- Ensure **DX (Developer Experience)** is as polished as UX.
- Provide **guardrails, not gates** — enable safe freedom.
- Measure success in **adoption, ecosystem health, and speed of innovation**.

---

## For Teams Using the Platform

- Treat the platform as a **partner, not a vendor**.
- Contribute feedback, extensions, and improvements back.
- Follow governance and shared standards — **your success strengthens the ecosystem**.
- Invest in learning the platform’s capabilities before rebuilding custom solutions.

---

## Objectives

Facilitate: by reducing complexity, removing what can be a pain for co-creators. 

Standardize: simplify and reduce stress, ensure safety, consistency and interoperability

## Success Metrics

**INDIRECT (feedback / NPS) **

- **Adoption rate**: Are teams and co-creators choosing the platform?
- **Ecosystem output**: How much new value is created by others on top?
- **Time-to-value**: How quickly can a new team build something meaningful ?

**DIRECT**:

- **Reliability**: Is the platform trustworthy as a foundation?
    - **Deployment Frequency;**
    - **Lead Time For changes;**
    - **Change Failure Rate;**
    - **Time to restore Services;**
- **Platform Empathy:** how the Platform Team understands, interprets, and addresses the needs of its co-creator? 
    - **Understanding: ****How well the team understands the customer’s problem before acting;**
    - **Responsiveness: How quickly the team provides a meaningful first response or proposal;**
    - **Solution Fit: How effectively the delivered solution solves the actual problem;**
    - **Collaboration Quality: How much customers feel involved in the process;**
